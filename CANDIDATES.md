# Candidate labels — Funniest Time Machine Warning Label

All ten candidates were drafted in the same build session, then scored
1–5 on four axes: **Surprise** (most surprising), **Specificity** (most
specific), **Concision** (most concise), **Judge-fit** (the competition's
own criteria from RULES.txt, in order: a real time-travel hazard turned
into the joke; surprise saved for the last few words; straight-faced
safety-notice tone; no wasted words). Max 20.

## Winner: #3

> WARNING: Passengers who kill their own grandfather will be charged a cleaning fee.

**Why it won (tie-break over #1, also 19):** both score full marks on
surprise and judge-fit, but #3's hazard is the canonical one — the
grandfather paradox, the textbook time-travel hazard — while #1's
"pregnant with yourself" is a paradox gag that names the paradox more
than it turns a hazard into a joke. #3 also lands its surprise in the
very last three words ("a cleaning fee"), a bureaucratic penalty for an
existence-ending crime, which is the straightest face of the lot: it
reads exactly like a real airline safety notice until the final clause.
#1's punchline ("yourself") is strong but arrives as a single-word
twist; #3's is a three-word rug-pull. Shorter is not better here —
criterion 5 rewards every phrase doing work, and in #3 every phrase
does: WARNING (notice tone) → Passengers (who it binds) → kill their
own grandfather (the hazard) → charged a cleaning fee (the joke).
